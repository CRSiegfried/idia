# Changelog

All notable changes to idia are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Before 1.0, a minor release may break the API; each break is listed under **Changed** or **Removed**. A new question kind, a new elicitation or a new validation rule is a minor release and is listed here.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- Project scaffolding: package, license, contributing notes, the spec stub, and the `live` test marker.
- The core vocabulary: every shape from `State` to `Response`, `Capabilities` with `Rounding` and `UNBOUNDED`, the closed kind and elicitation sets with their candidates, the answer union with `Fault` and `Rule`, `validate` and `validate_answers`, the `Leaf`, `Provider` and `TokenEstimator` protocols, the errors, and the provider registry behind its import guard; the spec's Vocabulary section.
- The scripted leaf, `Scripted` in `idia.providers.scripted`, with `Reply`, `ScriptedCall` and `ScriptExhausted`: a leaf answered from a test's script or function, with a call log, validated answers and filled provenance; the spec's Test doubles section.
- Fit: preflight of a request against a provider's capabilities, greedy packing of overflowing questions into wire requests that repeat the state, `OverflowPolicy` with `Fail` as the default, `DoesNotFit` and the `overflow-policy` warning code; the spec's Fit section.
- Dispatch: `Dispatcher`, the one loop that runs a fit plan inside the caller's deadline with `RetryPolicy`, `ConcurrencyLimit` and `RateLimiter`, one outcome per wire request and the re-ask after a fault; `Router` and `Fallback` with their derived capabilities; `idia.provider(name, ...)`; the spec's Dispatch section.
- `aclose()` and async-context-manager use on `Dispatcher`, `Router` and `Fallback`: a dispatcher closes a leaf that has `aclose`, a composite closes each of its members once, so a provider built through `idia.provider` can release the client its leaf created.
- The conformance suite, `idia.conformance`: `leaf_checks` (L1 to L29) and `provider_checks` (P1 to P19) over a `LeafHarness` or `ProviderHarness` built from exchanges, the verdicts pass, fail or not applicable with `CheckNotApplicable`, and `run_checks` for use without a test runner; the scripted leaf, the dispatcher, the router and the fallback pass it; the spec's Conformance suite section.
- The recorded leaf, `Recorded` in `idia.providers.recorded`, with `ReplayMiss`: records a leaf's calls under the hash of the canonical wire request and replays them with no call, in modes `none`, `once` and `new`, from one JSON log per leaf configuration; the spec's Test doubles section.
- P19 runs: a wrapped leaf's calls, recorded and replayed through the loop, give the same `Response` or `RequestFailed` with no wire request; `ProviderHarness.leaf(exchanges)` builds the leaf it records; L10 allows an `except` naming `ProviderError` whose body ends in a bare `raise` outside any loop.
- The HTTP harness in idia's tests: `HttpLeafHarness` over `httpx2.MockTransport`, the cassette format, and `record_cassette` with its secrets filter; `httpx2` joins the dev dependencies.
- `HttpLeaf` in `idia.providers.http`, the base an HTTP leaf may inherit: an injected or created `httpx2` client, closed only when created, with no transport retries; the user agent; one POST under the deadline; a body read as a whole; and the status table behind `map_status`, with `retry-after-ms` read before `retry-after`.
- The System One leaf, `SystemOne` in `idia.providers.system_one`, registered as `system-one` and installed by the `system-one` extra (`httpx2`): `noul`, `choice` and `score` on every model, the `images` feature on every model and `box` with it on `jev-1.14.0` and `jev-preview`, and the `long_context` feature; its record per model and feature, its estimator, its provenance, and its cassettes; the spec's Providers and capabilities section.
- The OpenAI decisions leaf, `OpenAIDecisions` in `idia.providers.openai_decisions`, registered as `openai-decisions`: OpenAI's Decisions API through the `openai` SDK with its retries at zero, `noul` as a predicate, `choice` and `score` with their names echoed and checked, a per-question `Refusal`, the 200-question cap a caller may raise, its record, estimator, provenance and cassettes.
- The OpenAI chat leaf, `OpenAIChat` in `idia.providers.chat`, registered as `openai-chat`: label probabilities from a chat model on Chat Completions by the `logprobs` elicitation, one question per call, letter-coded labels, surface forms summed over the first token's top 20 logprobs, renormalized over the labels with the off-label mass recorded, the derived `chosen` and `expected_level` named in `derived`, a letter outside the top-k read as zero mass with the `WARNING_LABEL_ABSENT_FROM_LOGPROBS` warning naming the labels, each with its letter, and the ceiling on their mass, the smallest probability returned, and the `empty_logprobs`, `label_absent_from_logprobs` (only when every label's letter is missing) and `off_label_mass_above_bound` faults (the zero-mass reading changes recorded answers, as any change to the protocol does); a `capabilities` record that contradicts the protocol is refused; `max_off_label_mass` bounds the off-label mass; the spec's Elicitation section.
- `check_elicitation(name)`, public, which a chat leaf calls at construction.
- The `openai` extra (`openai>=3.35.1`, `httpx2>=2.16`), which installs both OpenAI leaves; `openai` joins the dev dependencies.
- The Cloudflare leaves in `idia.providers.cloudflare`, installed by the `cloudflare` extra (`httpx2`): `Clef`, registered as `cloudflare-clef`, for `noul`, `choice` and `score` on `clef` and `clef-flash`, with images in a top-level array; and `Clairic`, registered as `cloudflare-clairic`, for `extraction`, its fields from each question's value type and instruction, `reference_date` read from the request's options, a distribution in a reply a `candidate_shape` fault; Workers AI's envelope, its status mapper with the daily-quota 429 not retryable, their records, estimators, provenance and cassettes; each model is taken by its short name or its full name, `@cf/cloudflare/<model>`, and reported by the short one.
- `cloudflare(...)` in `idia.cloudflare`: a `Router` over Clef for the label kinds and Clairic for extraction, sharing one client and the dispatchers' limiters.
- `WARNING_PROVIDER_TRUNCATED` (`provider-truncated`): a leaf's report that the wire truncated the state; the answers stand.
- `Request.options` and `WireRequest.options`: per-request settings a leaf may read by key, carried unchanged to every wire request of the plan, the re-ask and each router member, and part of the recorded leaf's key when not empty; `WARNING_UNKNOWN_OPTION` (`unknown-option`) and `unknown_options(options, known)`, with which every leaf ignores an option it does not know and names it in one warning.
- The conformance suite's L29: an unknown option gives exactly one `unknown-option` warning naming it, the answers and the body sent unchanged.
- The conformance suite's L30, P20, P21 and P22, and a second P18 driver: a call after `aclose` raises a non-retryable `ProviderError` and sends nothing; a non-retryable 401, and a 403, stops each member's plan after its first call; a request whose deadline has passed sends nothing and fails with `DeadlineExceeded` outcomes of no attempts; a fallback whose first member fails with a 401 still tries the next, with what remains of the same deadline.
- The System One leaf refuses an image smaller than 280 pixels on either side before sending, with a non-retryable `ProviderError` naming the minimum and the image's size, read from a PNG's `IHDR` or a JPEG's start-of-frame without a new dependency; an image whose size cannot be read is sent. The minimum is the one `jev-1.14.0` refused with a 400, measured on PNGs; JPEG was not measured.
- `Deadline.passed()`: whether the deadline has passed or is within one tick of the monotonic clock, `max(resolution, ulp(now))`, the most by which asyncio fires a timer early; the dispatcher and `within` read the deadline by it.
- The dispatcher's concurrency guarantee, stated in the spec's Dispatch section: one `Dispatcher` may serve several `answer` calls at once on one event loop, each with its own plan, deadline, retries and outcomes, sharing its leaf, concurrency limit and rate limiter; and a `Fallback`'s carried attempts and time land on the answering member's first outcome, answered or failed, its other outcomes keeping their own.
- The test HTTP harness's `write_cassette` and `write_run_cassettes` take `recorded_with`, written as the cassette's optional `recorded_with` member. The cassette format stays 1.
- The test HTTP harness's `record_cassette` takes `check(side, result)`: the cassette is written first to a side file outside the cassette tree and reaches its path only when the check returns, so a live run that stops leaves the committed cassette as it was; the Cloudflare, OpenAI and System One live runs record through it.
- `Dispatcher.plan(request)`: the preflight and fit plan alone, with no call, returning the `FitPlan` that `answer` then runs, or raising `DoesNotFit`, so code outside idia can check that a request fits before sending it; `FitPlan` is exported from `idia`. `Dispatcher.reask` and `Dispatcher.overflow` are read-only properties giving what the dispatcher was built with. A `Router` or `Fallback` has no `plan`.
- `Usage.passes`: the number of passes the wire reports for the call, `None` where it reports none; Clairic fills it from its `usage`, every other shipped leaf leaves it `None`, and the wire's own key stays in `raw_usage`. The recorded leaf keeps it in its log and leaves it out when `None`, so a log recorded before it replays unchanged.
- `Capabilities.max_image_bytes`, `max_total_image_bytes`, `min_image_side` and `image_media_types`: each image's raw bytes, all the images' bytes, the fewest pixels on a side and the media types an image may have. Fit checks each image of a state against them before any call, reading the type from the declared `media_type` or else the PNG, JPEG or WebP signature and the size from the PNG or JPEG header, and names every off feature in the `DoesNotFit`; an unknown field, or a size it cannot read, is an `unchecked-limit` warning on a request with an image. A composite shows them, derived over its members that take images. The conformance suite's L31 checks a leaf's image fields agree with its record, and P23 that an image past a known field is refused before any call.
- `noul_complement(probability)`: a `noul`'s probability of no, the exact decimal difference `1 - probability` converted to the nearest float, unrounded; it refuses a value that is not a number with `TypeError` and one not finite or outside 0 to 1 with `ValueError`. The conformance suite's L21 now also fails a leaf whose module refers to it, since a leaf hands over the probability of yes alone.
- `scripts/check_terms.py --subjects <range>` applies the terms check's patterns to the subjects of the commits in a git revision range; CI runs it over the commits a pull request or a push to main brings.

### Changed

- The binding to ommat ships with ommat, as `ommat[idia]`; idia has no dependency on ommat.
- The spec states Clef's refusal of a question id outside its pattern as recorded on `clef`: 422 with code 3030, its message quoting the pattern, where it had stated 400 and code 5006.

- A leaf's `Capabilities` refuses a `max_images`, `max_images_per_question`, `max_image_bytes` or `max_total_image_bytes` of 0 beside the image modality, which would refuse every image; a composite's derived record may hold one.
- `Capabilities` has four more fields with no default, the image fields; a record built by hand must state them, 0, 0, 0 and `frozenset()` for one without images. A media type in `image_media_types` must be lower-case and canonical, `image/jpeg` not `image/jpg`.
- The OpenAI decisions leaf takes PNG and JPEG only: a GIF or WebP image, declared or read from its bytes, now does not fit and is refused before any call, where it had been sent.
- `idia.provider` has a typed overload per registered name, `Literal` names with that leaf's constructor keywords and `retry`, `concurrency` and `rate`, and a last overload for any `str`; a registered name called with a keyword its leaf does not take, or a value of the wrong type, matches a deprecated catch overload, so pyright in strict mode reports it (in standard mode, and under mypy without its `deprecated` error code, it is a hint only). System One's models are typed by `SystemOneModel` in `idia.providers.system_one`. The overloads name no SDK type, `client` typed `object`, so a program without an extra type-checks. A keyword the leaf does not take raises `TypeError` as `provider('<name>'): ` and the leaf's message, the leaf's error as its cause, and for `estimator`, `overflow` or `reask` a pointer to building the `Dispatcher` by hand; a `TypeError` the leaf raises about a value passes through unchanged.
- The spec states how to read a wire id: an outcome's `keys` are in the order its wire request sent them, `prompt_sent` is the supported view of what was sent, and the id at position *i* of a wire request stands for `keys[i]`.
- The shapes a caller builds, `Deadline`, `TextPart`, `ImagePart`, `State`, `Label`, `Question`, `Request` and `WireRequest`, type-check their fields and refuse an empty key, instruction, label name, label description or image media type, each error naming the field: `TypeError` "`<Class>.<field>` must be …, got `<type>`", `ValueError` "`<Class>.<field>` must not be empty". A tuple-valued field no longer accepts a list, and `options` must be a mapping, where a list of pairs had been converted. `TextPart.text` may be empty and `JsonPart.value` is not checked. `Deadline(at)` refuses a bool, a non-number, a non-finite instant and one more than 30 days ahead on the monotonic clock, the mark of a wall-clock time, and `Deadline.after(seconds)` refuses more than 30 days with its own message.
- The conformance suite's P12 runs whichever of its moved-sum and unreadable-body drivers the harness can make, and is not applicable only when it can make neither; L21 allows the chat leaf's off-label mass to compute `1 - x`. `HttpLeaf`'s body reading is the module functions `read_object` and `unreadable`, which the OpenAI leaves share; `openai-organization` and `openai-project` join the cassette secret headers.
- `ExtractionAnswer.p_null` is optional, `None` where the provider gives none, as for a list, and validation checks it only when present.
- `Span(part, start, end)`: the index of the text part and 0-based, end-exclusive code-point offsets into it, both required; `path` is removed.
- The conformance suite's P12 runs its unreadable-body driver even when the request holds no question whose sum can move.
- The test HTTP harness's `write_cassette` and `record_cassette` take `redact`, for a secret outside the headers, and `HttpLeafHarness.handler(exchanges)` returns the mock wire's handler alone.
- The Cloudflare records' `key_patterns` are empty: `Clef` and `Clairic` send a wire request's keys as given when the wire takes them all, and otherwise send every question of it as `q0..qN` or `f0..fN` and map the answers back to the caller's keys, so a key such as `q:match/intent` reaches them.
- `DoesNotFit` for `key_patterns` names every failing key in question order, each with the first pattern it fails: `observed` is the tuple of failing keys and `limit` the record's whole `key_patterns`.
- `idia.provider(name, ...)` is annotated as returning `Dispatcher`, the type it always built, not `Provider`.
- The test HTTP harness's `write_cassette` stores the request and response bodies verbatim, keys in the order sent and received, and the rest of the cassette with sorted keys; the match is unchanged, JSON value equality, and no committed cassette was re-recorded.
- The test HTTP harness's `replay_headers(headers)` is public: a stored response's headers without the length and encodings, as a replay sends them, which the leaves' test wires use in place of a private set.
- The test HTTP harness's cassette match compares key order: a request matches a cassette only when its body, parsed as JSON, equals the stored body with every object's keys and every array's items in the same order, compared by the public `body_key(body)`, so two bodies equal in value but in another order no longer answer for each other; the stub leaf's hand-written cassettes hold their bodies in the order the stub sends, and a probe cassette stored with sorted keys is replayed only by name.
- `RequestFailed`'s message names the first failed outcome's provider, model and error, and how many outcomes failed, as `no wire request answered: system-one jev-1.13.0: HTTP 401 (1 outcome failed)`; a response with no failed outcome gives its count alone, `(1 outcome)` or `(n outcomes)`.
- `Dispatcher(leaf)` checks its argument before reading it: a `Dispatcher`, `Router` or `Fallback` raises `TypeError` saying it is already a provider and belongs in a `Router` or `Fallback`, and anything else that is not a `Leaf` raises `TypeError` naming the members a leaf has, where both raised `AttributeError` on `estimator`.
- A leaf's client follows the running event loop: `HttpLeaf` and the OpenAI leaves make a fresh created client on another loop, dropping the old one unclosed, and `aclose` closes the current one or drops one last used on another loop, so one provider serves successive `asyncio.run` calls, as a synchronous wrapper around each call makes. An injected client called on another loop than its first raises a non-retryable `ProviderError` before anything is sent, and a leaf called after `aclose` raises `the leaf is closed`, a leaf whose client it created creating no further client; an injected client stays open. `follow_the_loop`, `on_this_loop`, `client_failed` and `OTHER_LOOP` join `idia.providers.http`.
- `ConcurrencyLimit` makes its semaphore for the running event loop and again on another, so a limiter serves successive `asyncio.run` calls; the limit holds within each loop.
- The `cloudflare()` router's Clairic sends with whichever client Clef holds for the running loop, so the router follows the loop over one client.
- The conformance suite's L11 also calls a leaf on a second event loop after a success there, in a worker thread: the call must end in a `WireResponse` or a `ProviderError`.
- `ProviderNotFound` has `registered`, the registered names sorted, and its message lists them: `no provider is registered as 'openai'; registered: cloudflare-clairic, cloudflare-clef, openai-chat, openai-decisions, system-one`.
- `Router` refusing a kind routed to a member whose record lacks it names the member and its kinds: `kind 'extraction' is routed to the Dispatcher over system-one (jev-1.13.0), whose record lacks it (its kinds: choice, noul, score)`; a composite member is named by its type.
- A wire request failing with a credential error, a non-retryable `ProviderError` with status 401 or 403, leaves the rest of that `answer` call's plan, and any re-ask, unsent: each unsent wire request's outcome carries the same error with no attempts and no request id, where every wire request was sent and refused in turn; a recording that held more than one such refusal for one call now replays only the first.
- `Capabilities` has two required fields, `feature_kinds` and `feature_modalities`: what each opt-in feature adds on the model, whether or not it is on. A record built by hand must now state both, empty where the model has no opt-in feature; the constructor refuses a key outside `opt_in_features`, a feature mapped to the empty set, and an enabled feature mapped to a value the record does not declare, and raises `TypeError` for a map that is not a mapping or a value that is not a set, as `Capabilities.feature_kinds['images'] must be a set, got str`. System One maps `images` to the image modality on every model and to `box` on `jev-1.14.0` and `jev-preview`; every other record in the package, the composites' included, has both maps empty.
- `DoesNotFit` for `kinds` or `modalities` is narrowed: `observed` is the missing values, not every value asked, and `off_features` only the features the record says add one, and the message says which, as `kinds not supported: box; the images feature adds it`, or that none does, as `kinds not supported: box; no feature of model jev-1.13.0 adds it`. A composite's own check says `no feature of this composite adds it; its members' own records may`, except a router's check for kinds, which says `this router routes it to no member`. A failure on any other check still names every off feature. Preflight takes the model's name, keyword-only, for that message; the Dispatcher passes its leaf's.
- The conformance suite's P6 expects the narrowed `observed` and `off_features` for a missing kind or modality, and L7 checks the feature maps do not change with what is enabled.
- The spec states what an unknown model name does on the decisions leaf: it is sent as given and answered 404 `model_not_found`, a non-retryable `ProviderError` that is not a credential error, so the plan's other wire requests are still sent; the cassette `unknown-model` records it.
- The spec states that the recorded leaf's key is computed over the canonical wire request, not the text the leaf sends, so a leaf whose serialisation changes replays what its old text drew: delete its logs and record them again, since no mode replaces a recording its key already holds.
- The spec states the Cloudflare neighbour measurement: adding to a request one question whose key sorts first moved every other question's probability by up to 0.13 on Clef and none on `openai-decisions`, more than reversing four questions did (0.053).
- The spec states how the decisions leaf writes a JSON part, compact JSON with its keys in the value's own order, and what was measured of it: on `gpt-6-luna` one `noul` question answered 0.83 through the leaf's compact text and 0.62 with `json.dumps`' default separators by direct call, so a recording made with other serialisation does not stand for a request sent through the leaf.

### Fixed

- `validate` faults a bool or any value that is not a number in a probability, confidence, `p_null` or box edge as `not_finite`, observed as its type's name; a bool passed as 1.0 or 0.0, and a string made `validate` raise.
- A wire request whose next retry the deadline cannot fit, its delay or a limiter's wait ending past the deadline or the deadline passing before the call, now ends with `DeadlineExceeded`, saying the deadline ended it, its failed attempts listed with the provider's errors and the outcome's own `request_id` `None`; it had carried the last retryable error, which read the same as a plain failure.
- A request the client refuses before anything is sent because it is malformed, an illegal header value such as a key with a trailing newline, a key that cannot be encoded, a base URL with no or another scheme or one that cannot be parsed, is a non-retryable `ProviderError` naming the error's class, where it had been retried until the deadline or had escaped as another exception; an illegal header or an unencodable value is named by its class alone, since its text quotes the key.
- A `ProviderError`'s message, and so its `str()` and any `RequestFailed` that quotes it, no longer carries a key-shaped substring: an `sk-` API key or a bearer token written out, as a wire's error body or a transport error quoting the `Authorization` header can, is replaced by `<filtered>` when the error is built, and a body's text is filtered before it is cut short so no piece of a key survives the cut. The request id and the other fields are kept as given.
- A leaf refuses a client that reports itself closed, an `httpx2.AsyncClient` whose `is_closed` or an `AsyncOpenAI` whose `is_closed()` is true, before sending, with a non-retryable `ProviderError`, `the client is closed`, where the closed client's bare `RuntimeError` had been a retryable error the dispatcher retried. The retryable mapping of a bare `RuntimeError` now covers only one raised mid-call, a connection that died under the client.
- A Clairic sharing Clef's client, as `cloudflare()` builds them, refuses its calls after its own `aclose` while Clef is still open, where it had gone on sending.
- A leaf no longer refuses an image inside its call, where the refusal cost a failed attempt with nothing sent: System One's 280 px minimum and PNG-or-JPEG rule, Clef's and Clairic's media types, image count and 4 and 8 MiB caps, and the OpenAI decisions leaf's untyped image are each declared in the record and refused by Fit before any call. A leaf called directly with such an image sends it for the wire to judge.
- `Deadline.after(-math.inf)` is refused with `Deadline.after`'s own message, as `inf` and `nan` are, where it had reached `Deadline(at)` and been refused as a non-finite instant; the message now reads "Deadline.after takes a finite number of seconds, at most 30 days (2592000 seconds), got …".
- A leaf no longer lets a bare `RuntimeError` from its client out, as httpx2 and the `openai` SDK do when a pooled connection's event loop has closed: `HttpLeaf` and the OpenAI leaves raise a retryable `ProviderError` whose message names it; a subclass propagates. Such a call may have reached the wire.
- A second `decide_sync`, or `asyncio.run(provider.aclose())` after one, over a provider whose leaf created its client no longer fails with `Event loop is closed`; a contended `ConcurrencyLimit` no longer fails on a second loop.
- A call cut at its deadline by an asyncio timer, which may fire up to one clock tick early (about 16 ms on Windows before Python 3.13), no longer reads as having time left: the dispatcher does not retry it, so a retry policy with no delay no longer adds a failed attempt for a call that never reached the wire, and the outcome keeps the cut's error; a wire request within one tick of its deadline is not sent, its outcome `DeadlineExceeded` with no attempts, and `within` refuses it before awaiting.
- P11 accepts an outcome left unsent by a credential error: no attempts, no request id, and the very error object of an earlier outcome, not retryable with status 401 or 403; it had required every outcome to list a failed attempt or be `DeadlineExceeded`, so a correct dispatcher failed it when a 401 split plan stopped. The spec's `Failure` exchange states that `Failure()` is not a credential error, and idia's tests guard that no HTTP harness records its failure as a 401 or 403.
- The dispatcher checks the deadline again after the limiter waits, so a wait that ends past the deadline, as a late wake of the rate limiter or an uncontended concurrency limit can, no longer calls the leaf: before the first attempt the wire request is unsent with `DeadlineExceeded` and no attempts, before a retry it fails with the last error, and the slot is given back. An HTTP leaf had refused the late call itself, counted as a failed retryable attempt.
- The chat leaf's `logprobs` read sums its masses with `math.fsum`, so its answers are bit-identical on every CPython version: the `off_label_mass` that differed between 3.11 and 3.12+ came from the leaf's own float summation, not from the wire's floats.
- The recorded leaf writes its log with LF line endings on every platform; on Windows text mode had written CRLF, so a log's bytes differed by platform.
- The test HTTP harness's `write_cassette` writes through a temporary file beside the cassette, replaced in one step, so a failed write leaves an earlier cassette as it was, or none, and no partial file.
- The Cloudflare status mapper treats code 4006, which the wire answered when the daily free allocation of neurons ran out, as a quota spent beside 3036: not retryable on any status, where a 429 with 4006 had been retried.
- The Cloudflare live module records the candidate-estimate cassette once, never overwriting it, and writes it only when the wire's 200 answers the field with an estimate.
- The test HTTP harness's `record_cassette` raises `NoExchange` when the leaf made no exchange, as on a timeout or a failed connection, so the Cloudflare live module stops on it as no evidence; a Cloudflare model gone is kept as `gone.json`, never overwritten, and no longer overwrites Clef's failure cassette.
